# Help map campus with Campus Mesh

Campus Mesh is an offline chat and campus app for GCTU. We're building 3D views of campus buildings from ordinary phone videos, and you can help by filming one.

## How to contribute a scan

1. Read the [capture guide](CAPTURE_GUIDE.md). It takes 2 minutes and makes the difference between a usable scan and a blurry one.
2. Film the building, corridor or room.
3. Upload the video somewhere we can download it: Google Drive, OneDrive or WeTransfer, with link sharing turned on.
4. **[Submit your scan](https://github.com/Dankwa-Wadie/campus-mesh-community/issues/new?template=place-scan.yml)** with the link and the place you filmed.

We review every video before using it. Once your scan is used in the map, your name is added to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

## What happens to your video

- We use it to rebuild the place in 3D on a computer. The app then ships small images and better building positions made from it.
- We don't publish your raw video.
- Videos showing people's faces up close, or private rooms, are rejected and deleted.
- By submitting, you agree your video can be used for the Campus Mesh map under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with credit to the name you give.
