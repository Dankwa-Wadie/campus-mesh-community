# Capture guide

Good scans come from slow, steady video with lots of overlap between frames.

**Do**
- Film in daylight, or in a well-lit corridor.
- Walk slowly, about half your normal pace.
- Hold the phone upright and steady, at chest height.
- **Outside:** walk a full circle around the building, keeping it in frame the whole time.
- **Inside:** walk each corridor end to end, then turn around and walk it back.
- Keep each clip between 30 and 90 seconds.
- Film at 1080p. 4K is not needed.

**Don't**
- Pan quickly or swing the phone around.
- Film people's faces up close, or point the camera into offices or classrooms that are in use.
- Zoom in or out.
- Use filters, portrait mode or stabilisation effects.

**Bonus:** in the submission form, mention which floor you were on and which entrance you started from. It helps us place indoor scans correctly.
